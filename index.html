03 / Work

Featured build

Tessera

A multi-tenant help desk built for managed service providers

Tessera is a full-stack support desk SaaS I designed and built from scratch: multiple client organizations, isolated by row-level security, sharing one Postgres database safely. It handles the whole support loop (AI ticket triage, SLA tracking, threaded replies, and a branded client portal) on real background job processing instead of blocking requests.

  • Multi-tenant isolation enforced with Postgres row-level security, checked by an automated isolation test suite
  • AI ticket triage and reply drafting on Groq, running on a background job queue with retries
  • Business-hours-aware SLA tracking with automatic breach detection and email digests
  • Branded self-service client portal with magic-link auth and per-client theming
  • Stripe subscription billing with plan-based usage limits
